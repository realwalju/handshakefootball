THE BASICS

Play the space.

You control the player with the ring. Your teammates offer passing angles, run into channels, press, and cover behind the ball.

Move: WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to sprint. On a phone, use the joystick and action buttons.

Pass: aim with your movement direction and tap J. Control switches to the receiver, who runs to meet the ball until you steer. Your first touch briefly settles the ball; tap your next pass during it to queue the action. L sends a through ball ahead of them.

Shoot: hold K to charge, then release. Aim with movement; when standing still, the shot aims toward goal.

Defend: Q switches to a defender near the ball. E commits a standing tackle at the exposed ball. A successful challenge knocks it loose; allow time to recover before trying again. Goalkeepers play automatically.

3D view: the broadcast camera follows the ball and controlled player. Use the pitch map to see wider runs, or Camera for the full pitch. 2D is also available. Movement, passing, and switching use the same keys.

Online: create a room and share its code or invite link. The match pauses if either connection drops. Both players vote to rematch.

Three-minute matches. Simplified restarts. No offside, fouls, substitutions, or aerial play in this prototype.
